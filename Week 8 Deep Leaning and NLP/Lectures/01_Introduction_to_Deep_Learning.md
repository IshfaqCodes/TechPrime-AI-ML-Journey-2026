# 1. Introduction to Deep Learning
### Deep Learning ka Ta'aruf

---

## What is Deep Learning? / Deep Learning Kya Hai?

- Deep learning is a **subset of Machine Learning**, which is a subset of **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**.
  *Deep Learning, **Machine Learning** ka ek subset hai, jo khud **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** ka subset hai.*
- It uses "biologically inspired" neural networks with many layers (hence "deep") to learn representations directly from data.
  *Ye "biologically inspired" neural networks use karta hai jinme kai layers hoti hain (isi liye "deep" kehte hain) taake data se seedha representations seekh sakay.*
- Unlike traditional methods, deep learning algorithms automatically discover the features needed for tasks like classification.
  *Traditional methods ke bar-aks, deep learning algorithms khud-ba-khud wo features dhoond letay hain jo classification jaisay tasks ke liye zaroori hotay hain.*

---

## Why is Deep Learning Important & Popular Now? / Deep Learning Ahem Kyun Hai aur Aaj Kal Popular Kyun Hai?

1. **Big Data:** We live in the age of big data, and these algorithms need massive amounts of data to be effective.
   *Hum big data ke daur mein rehte hain, aur in algorithms ko mo'assir hone ke liye bohot zyada data chahiye hota hai.*
2. **Hardware:** Modern GPUs and powerful hardware can handle the vast computational power these algorithms require, which wasn't available decades ago.
   *Modern GPUs aur powerful hardware wo zabardast computational power handle kar saktay hain jo in algorithms ko chahiye — pehlay yeh mumkin nahi tha.*
3. **Open-Source Software:** Building and deploying models is streamlined with tools like **TensorFlow** and **PyTorch**.
   *TensorFlow aur PyTorch jaisay tools ki wajah se model banana aur deploy karna aasan ho gaya hai.*

## Amazing Successes of Deep Learning / Deep Learning ki Hairat-Angaiz Kamyabiyan

- **1997:** IBM's Deep Blue defeated world chess champion Garry Kasparov.
  *IBM ke Deep Blue ne world chess champion Garry Kasparov ko haraya.*
- **2011:** IBM's Watson won the game show Jeopardy! against its champions.
  *IBM ke Watson ne game show Jeopardy! apnay champions ke khilaf jeeta.*
- **2015:** Google DeepMind's AlphaGo defeated 18-time world champion Lee Sedol at Go, a game vastly more complex than chess.
  *Google DeepMind ke AlphaGo ne 18 baar ke world champion Lee Sedol ko Go (chess se bhi zyada mushkil khail) mein haraya.*
- **Modern Applications:** Self-driving vehicles, fake news detection, earthquake prediction, and more.
  *Jadeed Applications: Self-driving vehicles, fake news detection, earthquake prediction, waghera.*

## The Core Idea / Bunyadi Idea

- **Traditional ML:** You manually define features for the computer (e.g., a face has eyes, a mouth; an eye has corners at a specific angle). This is tedious and complicated.
  *Aap computer ke liye khud features define kartay hain (misal: chehray par aankhen, munh hotay hain; aankh ka corner aik khaas angle par hota hai). Yeh mushkil aur pechida kaam hai.*
- **Deep Learning:** You feed raw data (e.g., images of faces) to the algorithm, and it learns its own hierarchical representation. It detects lines and edges, then combines them to detect eyes and mouths, and finally composes them to detect a face.
  *Aap raw data (misal: chehron ki tasveerain) algorithm ko dete hain, aur wo khud apni hierarchical representation seekh leta hai. Pehlay lines aur edges detect karta hai, phir unhein mila kar aankhen aur munh detect karta hai, aur aakhir mein chehra detect karta hai.*

---

## Key Takeaway / Yaad Rakhein

> Deep learning is powerful because it automates feature engineering. The machine learns *what* to look for, not just *how* to look for it.
>
> *Deep learning is liye powerful hai kyunke ye feature engineering ko automate kar deta hai. Machine yeh seekhta hai ke **kya** dekhna hai, sirf **kaisay** dekhna hai — yeh nahi.*
